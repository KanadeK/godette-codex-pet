# Direction QA resolution

The final atlas passed strict V2 structure validation and the two cardinal blind gates: 090 is screen-right versus 270 screen-left, and 000 is up versus 180 down. Three isolated blind reviews produced only review-level uncertainty on intermediate quarter turns; no hard gate failed.

Independent final visual QA judged the sixteen directions to be a readable clockwise loop. The continuity report's 180 transparent span is the natural space between the legs/lower pose, not a broken internal cutout. The larger transitions at 157.5 to 180 and 270 to 292.5 are visible turn changes, not scale, clipping, or identity breaks.

Release decision: accepted with the attached warnings retained as auditable evidence.
