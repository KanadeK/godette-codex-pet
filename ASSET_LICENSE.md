## Modifications

The character was redrawn as a compact sticker-like chibi for 192 x 208 Codex
pet cells. New animation poses, sixteen directional look poses, transparent
backgrounds, scale normalization, atlas assembly, and chroma cleanup were made
for this project. No upstream raster is copied into the final spritesheet.

Software code, documentation text, and CI configuration are not covered by this
art license; they use the repository's MIT License.
